About CoinAbacus

A small set of free calculators for people who trade or hold crypto.

What the site does

CoinAbacus answers the questions that come up before a trade: what will this make after fees, how big should the position be, where would it be liquidated, what is my real average cost, which exchange is cheapest, what would regular buying have returned, what does staking add up to, and what is this coin worth in my currency.

Each calculator shows its working. The formulas are written out on the page with an example you can check by hand, along with a list of what the calculation leaves out.

Where the numbers come from

  • Live prices come from CoinGecko's public market data and refresh once a minute. Daily price history for the DCA backtest comes from Binance's public spot market data. If the feed cannot be reached, the site says so and the calculators keep working with prices you type in. It never shows made-up prices.
  • Currencies. Every calculator except the DCA backtest works in any of 40 currencies, chosen from the menu at the top of the page. Amounts are converted with the same live exchange rates the converter uses.
  • Exchange fees are base-tier spot rates taken from each exchange's published fee schedule. The date they were last checked is shown above the table.
  • Everything else is arithmetic on the numbers you enter, done in your browser.

What it is not

CoinAbacus is not an exchange, a wallet or an adviser. It cannot place trades or hold funds, and nothing on it is a recommendation to buy or sell. Liquidation prices and backtests are estimates, and real results depend on your exchange, your order and the market at that moment.

Found a mistake?

If a result looks wrong or a fee is out of date, please send a message with the numbers you entered. Corrections are welcome.
