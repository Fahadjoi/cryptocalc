Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version

There are no accounts. The numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser and are not sent to this site or anyone else.

What is stored on your device

  • Your theme choice (light or dark), so the site looks the same next time.
  • Your portfolio, if you use the tracker: the coins, amounts and buy prices you enter.
  • A few settings you typed, such as your fee rate, balance and risk percentage, so you do not have to enter them again. Reset on a calculator clears them.
  • The most recent prices, kept for a few minutes so pages load quickly and the price feed is not asked more than needed.

All of it is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. Clearing site data removes them. No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

Outside services the site contacts

  • CoinGecko supplies prices. Your browser requests them directly, so CoinGecko sees your IP address and which coins were requested, but none of the amounts you enter.
  • Binance public market data supplies daily price history for the DCA backtest, requested the same way.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, and sees your IP address when the font is loaded.
  • Netlify hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.

Shared links

The "Copy link" buttons put the numbers from that calculation into the link itself. Anyone you send the link to can see those numbers, and so can any service the link passes through.

Messages you send

The contact form sends your name, message and optional email address to the site owner through Netlify's form service. They are used only to reply to you and to fix the problem you reported.

Advertising and analytics

The site does not currently run adverts or visitor analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated before they are switched on, and any consent required in your country will be asked for.
