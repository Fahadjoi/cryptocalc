Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version

There are no accounts. The numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser and are not sent to this site or anyone else.

What is stored on your device

  • Your theme and currency choice, so the site looks the same next time.
  • Your portfolio, if you use the tracker: the coins, amounts and buy prices you enter.
  • A few settings you typed, such as your fee rate, balance and risk percentage, so you do not have to enter them again. Reset on a calculator clears them.
  • The most recent prices, kept for a few minutes so pages load quickly and the price feed is not asked more than needed.

All of it is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. Clearing site data removes them. The site itself sets no cookies. Anything set by outside services is listed under Advertising and analytics below.

Outside services the site contacts

  • CoinGecko supplies prices. Your browser requests them directly, so CoinGecko sees your IP address and which coins were requested, but none of the amounts you enter.
  • Binance public market data supplies daily price history for the DCA backtest, requested the same way.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.

Shared links

The "Copy link" buttons put the numbers from that calculation into the link itself. Anyone you send the link to can see those numbers, and so can any service the link passes through.

Messages you send

If you email the site, your address and message are used only to reply to you and to fix the problem you reported.

Advertising and analytics

Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which calculators are used. Google sets cookies and receives your IP address, the pages you open and basic device details. It does not receive the numbers you type into a calculator.

Advertising. Adverts are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show adverts based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where the law requires consent, you are asked before these cookies are set.
