How to Use This Calculator

Enter the price you bought your crypto at, the price you sold (or plan to sell) at, how much USD you invested, and your exchange's fee percentage. Hit calculate and you'll instantly see your profit or loss.

What is a good crypto profit?

Any positive return is a profit. Most serious traders aim for a minimum 2:1 risk-reward ratio, meaning they target at least 2% gain for every 1% they risk. For long-term holders, annual returns of 20–50% are considered strong.

How exchange fees affect your profit

Exchange fees are charged twice — once when you buy and once when you sell. On a $1,000 trade at 0.1% fee each way, you pay $2 total. On a $10,000 trade, that's $20. These add up fast, especially for active traders. Binance charges 0.1%, Bybit 0.1%, Coinbase up to 0.6%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate crypto profit?

Formula: Profit = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Coins − Total Fees. This calculator does all of this automatically.

How are exchange fees calculated?

Exchange fees are usually a percentage of your trade value. Most exchanges charge 0.1%–0.2% per trade. You pay fees both when you buy and when you sell, so total fees = buy fee + sell fee.
