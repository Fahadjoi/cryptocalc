How to Size Your Crypto Positions

Position sizing is one of the most important — and most ignored — parts of crypto trading. Most beginners focus on finding good entries, but professional traders obsess over how much to risk on each trade.

The 1% rule

Many professional traders risk no more than 1-2% of their account on a single trade. With a $10,000 account, that's $100-200 per trade. This may sound small, but it means you can survive 50+ losing trades in a row before wiping your account.

How the calculator works

The formula is simple: Position Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ Stop-Loss Distance. If you have $10,000, risk 1%, and your stop is 5% below entry, your position is ($10,000 × 1%) ÷ 5% = $2,000. With leverage, your required margin is reduced but your risk stays the same.

Stop-loss placement

Always set your stop-loss before calculating position size, not after. Logical stop-loss levels are below key support zones, below recent swing lows, or at a percentage level that invalidates your trade idea.
