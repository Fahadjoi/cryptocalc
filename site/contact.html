Contact

Report a wrong result, an out-of-date fee, or suggest a calculator you would like to see.

Email

contact@coinabacus.com

Messages are read by the person who builds and maintains the site. Most get a reply within two working days.

What to include

  • A wrong result: the calculator, the numbers you entered and the result you expected. A copied link from the calculator holds all of them.
  • An out-of-date fee: the exchange, your account level and where the exchange publishes its current fee.
  • A missing coin or currency: its name and ticker, and the calculator you want it in.
  • A new calculator: the question you want it to answer.

What we cannot help with

CoinAbacus is a set of calculators, not an exchange, wallet or adviser. It cannot look up your account, recover funds, reverse a trade or tell you what to buy or sell. For problems with an account or a transfer, contact the exchange or wallet you used. See the About page for how the calculators and price data work.
