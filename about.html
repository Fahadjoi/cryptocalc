About CryptoCalc

CryptoCalc is a free collection of cryptocurrency calculator tools built for traders and investors. Our goal is simple: give you fast, accurate calculations with no signup, no paywalls, and no distractions.

What we offer

Free tools including profit/loss calculators, DCA calculators, exchange fee comparators, position size calculators, and crypto converters. All calculations run directly in your browser — we never store your data.
