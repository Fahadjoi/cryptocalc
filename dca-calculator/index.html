What is Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)?

Dollar cost averaging (DCA) is an investment strategy where you buy a fixed dollar amount of an asset at regular intervals — daily, weekly, or monthly — regardless of price. Instead of trying to time the market with one large purchase, you spread your buys over time.

Why DCA works in crypto

Crypto is highly volatile. When you DCA, you automatically buy more coins when prices are low and fewer when prices are high. Over time, this averages out your cost basis and reduces the risk of buying at the top. It's one of the most popular strategies among long-term Bitcoin and Ethereum holders.

How to use this DCA calculator

Enter how much you invest per period (daily, weekly, or monthly), how many periods you've been investing for, your average buy price, and today's current price. The calculator will show your total investment, how many coins you've accumulated, and your current profit or loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DCA in crypto?

DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) means buying a fixed dollar amount of crypto at regular intervals, regardless of price. It reduces volatility risk by spreading purchases over time rather than making one large buy.

Is DCA a good strategy for crypto?

Yes — DCA is widely considered one of the safest crypto strategies because it removes the need to time the market. By buying consistently, you automatically buy more when prices are low and fewer when they're high.

How often should I DCA into crypto?

Weekly or monthly DCA is most common and balances transaction fees against diversifying your entry points. Daily DCA gives the smoothest averaging effect but may incur higher fees depending on your exchange.
