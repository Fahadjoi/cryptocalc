How the tracker works

Each holding is the amount of a coin you own and, if you choose to give it, the average price you paid. The tracker multiplies each amount by the live price, adds them up, and converts the total into the currency you pick. Prices refresh once a minute while the page is open.

Value = amount × live priceProfit or loss = amount × (live price − average buy price)

The 24-hour figure shows how much the whole portfolio has moved since this time yesterday, using each coin's own 24-hour change. Profit and loss is only calculated for holdings where you entered a buy price, and the total says which holdings it covers.

Where your data is kept

Your holdings are saved in this browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent to a server, and there is no account to sign in to. That has three consequences worth knowing.

  • It is private. Nobody else can see what you hold, including the people who run this site.
  • It does not follow you. A different browser or device starts empty. Use Download backup on one and Restore backup on the other to move it.
  • Clearing browser data erases it. Download a backup now and then if the list took effort to build.

Getting the buy price right

If you bought a coin in several lots, the number to enter is your average cost: the total you spent divided by the total coins you received. The average cost calculator works it out from your individual buys. When you add more of a coin you already hold, the tracker merges the two and recalculates the average for you.

What it does not track

  • Coins outside the list. Twenty major coins are supported.
  • Trades and history. It shows where you stand now. It does not record past buys and sells or draw a value chart over time.
  • Wallets and exchanges. Nothing is connected, by design, so balances are whatever you type in.
  • Tax. Profit shown here is not a tax calculation.

Common questions

Is the portfolio tracker free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription and no limit on the number of holdings.

Where is my portfolio stored?

In your browser's local storage on the device you are using. It is not uploaded anywhere, which also means it will not appear on another device unless you move a backup file across.

How do I move my portfolio to my phone?

Press Download backup on the device that has it, send the file to your phone, then open this page there and press Restore backup.

Why is profit or loss blank for some coins?

Profit and loss needs a buy price. Type one into the Buy price column for that coin and it will appear.

Can I see my portfolio in rupees or another currency?

Yes. Pick a currency under Show values in. Fifteen currencies are available, including PKR, INR, AED, EUR and GBP.
