How to Convert Crypto to USD

Converting crypto to USD is straightforward: multiply the number of coins you hold by the current price. For example, 0.5 BTC at $65,000/BTC = $32,500. This converter does that math for any supported coin and currency pair.

What affects crypto prices?

Crypto prices fluctuate based on supply and demand, macroeconomic news, regulatory changes, and market sentiment. Bitcoin's price is also influenced by its fixed supply of 21 million coins. Unlike stocks, crypto markets never close — prices can move 24/7.

Crypto vs fiat conversion tips

When converting large amounts, be aware that the price you see on a converter may differ from the actual execution price on an exchange (this difference is called slippage). For amounts over $10,000, check the order book depth on your exchange before executing.
