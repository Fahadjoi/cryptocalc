Crypto Exchange Fee Comparison (2026)

Exchange fees vary significantly — Coinbase charges up to 0.6% while Binance charges just 0.1%. On a $10,000 trade, that's the difference between paying $60 and $10. Over a year of active trading, fees can easily cost you hundreds or thousands of dollars.

Maker vs taker fees

Most exchanges have two fee tiers: maker fees (you add liquidity by placing limit orders) and taker fees (you remove liquidity with market orders). Taker fees are usually higher. This calculator uses taker fees, which is what most traders pay when buying or selling at market price.

How to reduce exchange fees

Most exchanges offer fee discounts if you hold their native token (BNB on Binance, KCS on KuCoin). Higher trading volume also unlocks lower fee tiers. Using limit orders (maker orders) instead of market orders can halve your fees on some exchanges.
