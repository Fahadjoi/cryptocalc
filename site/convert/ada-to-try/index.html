How much is 1 ADA in TRY?

The live rate appears here once prices have loaded.

The figure is a market average taken across many exchanges, and it updates once a minute while this page is open.

How the ADA to TRY rate is worked out

Cardano is priced in US dollars on the world's exchanges. To show a value in Turkish lira, the converter takes that dollar price and multiplies it by the live exchange rate between the dollar and the Turkish lira.

ADA in TRY = amount × ADA price in USD × TRY per USD

What you will actually receive

A converter shows the market rate. When you really sell ADA for Turkish lira, three things reduce what arrives.

  • Trading fee. Usually 0.1% to 0.8% of the order. See the fee calculator for ten exchanges.
  • Spread. The buy and sell prices differ slightly, and the gap widens on quiet markets.
  • Withdrawal cost. Moving the money to a bank or wallet normally carries a fixed charge.

Prices on local peer-to-peer markets, where people trade ADA directly for Turkish lira by bank transfer, are set by local supply and demand. They can sit a few percent above or below the rate shown here, which is based on the official Turkish lira exchange rate.

Common questions

How do I convert ADA to TRY?

Type the amount of ADA in the first box. The value in Turkish lira appears in the second box at the live market rate. You can also type a TRY amount in the second box to see how much ADA it buys.

How often does the ADA to TRY rate update?

Once a minute while the page is open. The time of the last update is shown at the top of the converter.

Is this the rate my exchange will give me?

It will be close. Exchanges add a trading fee and a small spread, so you receive slightly less than the market rate shown here.

Can I convert TRY to ADA as well?

Yes. Press the swap button, or type into the second box, and the conversion runs the other way.
