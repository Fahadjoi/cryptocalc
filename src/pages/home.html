Fees counted on both sides

A trade pays a fee going in and another coming out. Every result here includes both, and shows the sell price you need just to break even.

Real prices, refreshed every minute

Entry prices fill in from the live market and the DCA backtest replays actual daily prices going back five years.

Your numbers stay with you

The maths runs in your browser. Balances and trade sizes you type are never uploaded or stored on a server.
